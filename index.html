09.40 – 10.45
Panel + Q&A

Moderator: Daniel Batten, CEO, CH4 Capital and Coach

Panel: Bastien Desteuque, MD, Institut National de Bitcoin & EBEA Board Member; Dr. Niall Burke, Lecturer & Researcher in Renewable Energies, Technological University of the Shannon; Aoife Foley, Chair of Net Zero Infrastructure, University of Manchester (tbc), Dr. Harald Rauter, Chief Growth Officer, 21Energy GmbH & EBEA Board Member.

Europe's energy transition was legislated into existence. Brussels chose the winners on the supply side, and hydro, solar and wind duly arrived. On the demand side we have far less clarity on who the winners are, with technological openness as the main guard rail. Volatility, curtailment and negative prices have turned system management into a live coordination problem, and a whole class of flexible demand has emerged to meet it. Batteries and storage are well understood. Industrial load shifting is maturing. Bitcoin mining is the one tool that utilities know least, despite being a controllable, interruptible load that monetises stranded power and can pay its way into grid stability.

There is no single European answer, because there is no single European market. A surplus problem in France is a capacity problem in Germany and a network problem in the Nordics. The rules that decide which flexible technology gets paid are still written nationally. The system will not crown one winner, it will need many, and the honest position today is that the jury is still out. The time to experiment, test and learn is now, and Europe is the place to do it.

10.45 – 11.15

Break & Coffee

11.15 – 12.00
Panel + Q&A

Moderator: Agustin Krupka, Mining Lead at Lunar Rails

Panel: Nacho Pauls, Chief Commercial Officer, Ocean LLC; Julie Landrum, OpenNode; Fabian Weber, Head of Hashrate, terahash.energy GmbH & Co-founder, Ourpool; Jesse Pielke, CEO HashrateUp

A deep dive into the economics and financial mechanics of Bitcoin mining—from a brief look at what Bitcoin mining is and how it works, to energy costs and power agreements to hardware investment, hosting models, financing and mining rewards.

Following the journey from megawatts consumed to revenue generated, this panel brings together operators and industry experts to explore the realities of building, scaling and managing mining businesses in an increasingly competitive global market.

12.00 – 12.45
Panel + Q&A

Moderator: Daniel Batten, CEO, CH4 Capital and Coach

Panel: Alexander Neumueller, Research Lead at Cambridge Centre for Alternative Finance; Dr. Harald Rauter, Chief Growth Officer, 21Energy; Juan Ibanez (tbc), Executive Director, MiCA Crypto Alliance, Javier Martin, Head of Energy, Mining and AI, Braiins

Bitcoin mining has become a truly global industry, shaped by energy markets, regulation, infrastructure and technological innovation. As the search for reliable, competitive and increasingly renewable energy sources accelerates, attention is increasingly turning towards Europe.

This panel explores the changing global mining landscape, emerging opportunities in Europe, regional trends and the forces shaping where the next generation of mining infrastructure will be built.

12.45 – 14.00

Lunch & Networking

14.00 – 14.45
Panel + Q&A

Moderator: Jesse Pielke, CEO Hashrate Up

Panel: Roni Karjalainen, CEO Minerssloop; Bipin Patel, CEO Flexionics Energy; Jaakko Kaijaala, CEO Nordblock; Charlie Fritzsche, Head of Customer Success, terahash.energy GmbH

As Europe increases its reliance on renewable energy, flexible electricity demand will become critical to balancing the grid. Bitcoin mining is uniquely capable of rapidly adjusting its power consumption, and thereby helps to absorb surplus generation, reduce curtailment and support grid stability. Local policy decisions, including changes to energy taxation and levies, can have a major impact on the economics of deploying flexible data centres and may determine whether these assets become part of the solution or remain unrealised opportunities. For energy and mining companies alike, understanding the value of flexibility—and the conditions needed to enable it—will be essential in Europe's changing energy landscape.

This panel examines how flexible data centres can complement Europe's evolving electricity system, while exploring the market structures and regulatory frameworks needed to unlock their full potential.

14.45 – 15.30
Panel + Q&A

Moderator: Harald Rauter, EBEA Board Member

Panel: Robert Schulmann, Board Chair, Minerssloop; Johnny Yao, Vice-president, Canaan Inc.; Kasimir Kaitue, Co-founder Nordblock

Heating and cooling represent approximately half of the EU's energy consumption, yet much of that demand still relies on fossil fuels. As Europe accelerates the decarbonisation of heat, integrating flexible computing with heat reuse represents an opportunity that neither the energy sector nor the mining industry can afford to overlook.

This panel explores how Bitcoin mining can transform waste heat into a valuable resource for homes, businesses, district heating and industry, improving energy efficiency while supporting Europe's transition to cleaner, more resilient energy systems.

15.30 – 16.15

Coffee & Networking

16.15 – 16.45
Keynote

Alexander Przybylla, CEO of Mentron GmbH & Agri PV Farmer

The antagonist isn't Bitcoin skepticism, but the European energy reality: expiring feed-in tariffs, negative electricity prices, curtailment, and long waiting lists for grid connections. In 2026 alone, 66,000 German PV installations are reaching the end of their 20-year EEG subsidy support, representing around 3.9 GW of capacity over the next 12 months. The bigger waves are still to come.

Alex's keynote takes us on a brutally honest learning journey of one family's path to work out what it takes to turn stranded solar energy into money & more in the South of Germany.

Closing Statement

Rachel Geyer, Board Chair EBEA
